About Office Addicts

The Office Addicts community is the largest fan group on Facebook dedicated to the American sitcom The Office. Founded by Jose Vivanco as a way to connect with fellow fans who watched the show every night before falling asleep, the group has grown into a bustling community. As of early 2025 it boasted more than 219,000 members, with over 197,000 active in a given month【625474364510990†L103-L116】.

It’s “more than just a Facebook group” — around 17 to 20 volunteers moderate posts around the clock, approving between 700 and 1,000 posts every day and welcoming 7,000 to 8,000 new members each week【625474364510990†L120-L126】. The group fosters a sense of comfort and belonging; many members say it’s the best part of their day【625474364510990†L136-L139】. Despite its size, the group doesn’t run ads and is managed entirely by fans out of love for the show【625474364510990†L164-L168】.

Office Addicts hosts live trivia nights, social meetups and lively discussions about episodes, characters and behind‑the‑scenes details. Whether you’re a casual viewer or a superfan who quotes every line, you’ll find plenty to enjoy and a community of friends who share your obsession.
